---
name: meeting-bot
description: "Use when the user texts 'join <Meet or Zoom link>', asks to send or remove Hermes's notetaker for a call, to turn its chat replies on or off, to say something in the meeting chat, to make it leave, asks whether it is in a call, asks for a whiteboard in the call (start a whiteboard, add X to the board, make it a timeline, stop sharing the board, turn the board link off, a new or watch-only board link), or asks about spoken commands in a call or what they cost."
---

The notetaker joins Google Meet and Zoom calls, records them, and files the transcript like any Hereby recording. Run `meeting-bot` (it prints JSON) and tell the user the result in one plain sentence. On an error, relay its message.

The moment it is let into a call, the notetaker posts its notice in the meeting chat (`notetaker_notice` in `~/.config/hereby/recorder.toml`), with chat replies on or off. It records, listens for spoken commands and keeps the chat only once the chat shows the notice. When it cannot post it (a host who turned chat off), the user gets one text saying it is in the call but not recording; it keeps trying every minute, and a second text says when it posted after all. Never change or skip the notice, and never tell the user it is recording when `meeting-bot status` says otherwise.

- "join <link>": `meeting-bot join '<link>'`. Add `--chat` only when they ask for chat replies in the same message. Join only a link they sent in this message. Only Google Meet and Zoom links work.
- "leave" or "drop out": `meeting-bot leave`. With several calls running, add `--event <key>`.
- "is the notetaker in?": `meeting-bot status`.
- "send Hermes to <meeting>", "don't send Hermes", or "chat replies on/off for <meeting>": find the event's `key` by its title or start time in `events` and `next` of `~/.local/state/live-meeting/schedule.json`. Then run `meeting-bot optin <key>`, `meeting-bot optout <key>`, or `meeting-bot chat <key> on|off`. Chat replies need the call sent first. A call joined by link gets chat replies only through `--chat` at join.
- "say <text> in the meeting": `meeting-bot say '<text>'`, with their words exactly. Never post anything from their notes in a meeting chat.
- Whiteboard ("start a whiteboard for the launch", "add a column for risks", "group these by owner", "make it a timeline", "new board", "show board 1", "stop sharing the board", "start a retro", "Lee owns the designer hire, due Friday", "undo", "recap"): `meeting-bot board '<their words>'`, their words exactly; add `--event <key>` with several calls running. It prints the board's number, title, whether it is on screen, and `board_url`, the call's live board link. Tell them in one sentence, for example "Board 1, Launch plan, is on screen in the call." Give them the link only when they ask for it; anyone with it can change the board. When `share_error` is set, say the board was drawn but the call would not show it, and why. Put nothing from their notes on a board.
- Board link ("turn off the board link", "kill the link", "new board link", "a view-only link"): `meeting-bot board-link off`, `meeting-bot board-link new`, or `meeting-bot board-link view`; add `--event <key>` with several calls running. `off` stops the link at once and cuts off everyone using it, its short link and QR code too; the shared screen still shows the board. `new` does that and prints `board_url`, a new link to give them. `view` prints `view_url`, a link that can watch but not change the boards, and leaves the board link working. Never post either new link in the meeting chat; once they turn the link off or make a new one, Hermes posts no board link there. Give the new link only to them, and say anyone with an edit link can change the boards.

The whiteboard is off until someone asks for one. Boards by name go up at once: an agenda, a decision log, action items, pros and cons, a timeline, a retro, or plain "a whiteboard", the meeting board of goals, risks, action items and decisions. Action items carry an owner and a due date (one with no owner is marked "No owner"), risks a level, and decisions show as the board's "Decided:" line. "Undo" (or "no, not that") takes back the last change. "Recap" puts up the call's action items, decisions and open risks; the bot also does this as it leaves, so the recap is in the notes, and with chat replies on it says the recap in the chat. Anyone in the call can also type "Hermes, start a whiteboard" or "Hermes, add X to the board" in the meeting chat, with chat replies on or off. The bot shares its screen with the board and draws each change as it is made: the status bar at the bottom shows what it heard within about a second, then says what changed in words, and Hermes's cursor points at it. The first board of a call takes a few seconds longer, while the share starts. Every board is saved into the meeting's notes after the call, as a picture, an editable Excalidraw file and its text; its action items and decisions join the note's own. Anyone with the board link can watch it live and change it (click a note, a section or the title); their pointer and name show on the shared screen, and Hermes's next change starts from their edits. With chat replies on, the bot posts the link in the meeting chat once, with the first board, and the shared screen then shows a QR code and a short link to it. The link stops working when the call ends.

People can also say it out loud in the call: "Hermes, put the launch dates on a timeline." The bot listens for a sentence that starts with "Hermes" while it is in a call with someone else, and a spoken board request changes the board like a typed one, usually starting within 2 seconds of the speaker stopping; the caption strip shows the words while they are still being said. With chat replies on, the chat also gets "Got it: <request>" at once, and a question said to Hermes ("Hermes, what did we decide about pricing?") is answered by text like a typed one; with them off, spoken questions are ignored. If speech recognition stops working in a call, or reaches its 4-hour limit for one call, the user gets one text saying so, the board's status bar says "Not listening, type in chat", and typed requests in the chat still work. Listening costs about $0.0061 per minute of call (Deepgram): `meeting-bot status` shows `voice_minutes` for each call, and `~/.local/state/meeting-bot/voice-usage.jsonl` has one line per call with its minutes.

What the notetaker keeps about a call (its chat, call audio, whiteboard pictures, logs, usage line and calendar opt-in on this machine, and the call's online board room) is removed within a minute of the user forgetting that meeting's notes, and otherwise after the recorder's `retention_days` in `~/.config/hereby/recorder.toml` (14 days by default). Notes already written into the vault are not removed by this.

With chat replies on, the user asks a question in the meeting chat, the once-a-minute check texts them the answer, and the meeting chat gets only "I texted you the answer." Teams and other links are refused.
